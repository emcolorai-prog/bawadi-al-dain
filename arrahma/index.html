About Us — Mauritania

Agriculture and livestock in Mauritania

Arrahma pour la Production Agricole et Animale is a Mauritanian limited liability company, established in Nouakchott in 2023.

Its programme covers forage, livestock breeding and fattening, animal feed, veterinary care, slaughtering and dairy, developed in stages.

Arrahma is the Mauritanian company of the Bawadi Al Dain group. Its integrated model covers livestock production, crop cultivation, feed production, slaughter facilities and related value-chain infrastructure.

Who We Are

Built on more than 35 years of experience

35+

Our story began with the founding of Arrahma pour la Production Agricole et Animale in Mauritania, where a dream became reality through vision, hard work and the challenge to excel. We started with small, deliberate steps towards our goals, and these soon became steady progress and rapid implementation.

The story is embodied in our founder, the businessman Muhammad Hamid Abkar, who holds the majority of the company’s shares with his family.

Founder and Chairman of the Board

Muhammad Hamid Abkar

One of Sudan’s leading businessmen in agriculture, livestock production and related manufacturing. Over a career of more than 35 years he has built a large, well-established business with a direct impact on the development of agriculture and livestock in Sudan.

His achievements in Sudan

  1. i

    Buying, gathering, raising and fattening livestock and supplying it to large companies and institutions, including Alategahat Almtadeda Co. Ltd, a Sudanese government company and a leading exporter of meat and livestock to Egypt, whose exports have passed 500,000 head in recent years

  2. ii

    Thousands of acres of crops in rain-fed areas such as Wadi Al-Daein, Darfur, Kordofan and Al-Jazira, and in irrigated areas: peanuts, cotton and forage crops

  3. iii

    A farm for the genetic improvement of local cattle through artificial insemination with frozen semen imported from abroad

  4. iv

    Large farms for cattle, sheep and camels in Al-Nahud, Darfur and Omdurman

  5. v

    Peanut collection and sorting centres that export first-grade peanuts, and two oil mills, in Al-Obeid and in Al-Baqir near Khartoum, whose oil is exported and whose cake is sold at home and abroad

  6. vi

    Social and humanitarian work in his home region, through partnerships with small herders and support for building schools and hospitals

Vision and Values

Our vision

Arrahma’s vision in Mauritania is strategic: to lay the first stone in renewing and developing agriculture and livestock production, in cooperation with the competent authorities. With a firm commitment to excellence, sustainability and innovation, the company aims to be a leading force in the country’s agriculture and livestock sector.

It pursues this through several large-scale projects in crop and livestock production, feed manufacturing, slaughterhouses for red meat, and milk and dairy products, to meet local and international needs.

Livestock and Crops

An integrated farm at Boghé and Aleg

On land acquired in 2023 in the Boghé/Aleg area of the Brakna region, Arrahma is building a livestock quarantine and production facility alongside forage and commercial crops.

50,000

head of cattle, sheep and camels: the design capacity of the livestock facility

Priority crops

  • Peanuts
  • Soybeans
  • Watermelon seeds
  • Sorghum
  • Maize

Grown under rain-fed and irrigated systems. The first rain-fed farming has produced encouraging results.

Alongside forage, the company has bought several thousand fruit seedlings, including mango and lemon, for planting on the project land, and has imported the machinery needed for farming and livestock production.

Processing and exports

Next steps include edible-oil production, peanut processing and animal-feed manufacturing, with exports to neighbouring markets.

The company is extending its farmland so it can use the water of Lake Aleg, and hopes a dam can be built to hold water through the dry season.

The second site: Hodh Ech Chargui

On about 1,000 hectares acquired with local breeders, the second strategic quarantine site will have pens for raising and fattening cattle, sheep and camels. Land levelling, fencing and site planning are under way.

Plans include developing rangeland on rain-fed land with drought- and salt-tolerant forage seed, wells to use groundwater in the dry season, and ponds that store rainwater for supplementary irrigation.

What the project is expected to bring

  • Work for hundreds of people: farm and livestock workers, administrators and technicians
  • Dozens of machines, from tractors and loaders to seeders, harvesters and balers, with drivers, technicians and mechanics
  • A more active livestock market in the region
  • Veterinary care and technical advice for local breeders
  • Modern forage farming that raises yields and improves animal health and growth
  • An incentive for breeders to raise more, healthier animals to supply the site, which will raise and fatten tens of thousands of head a year
